A Better Choice Education Program

Address: 135 Paseo Del Prado Suite 13, Edinburg, TX 78539

Phone: (956) 359-6380

DWI Education – $65 (12 hrs/3 days): First-time-offender DWI education covering the legal consequences of impaired driving.

DWI Intervention – $250 (32 hrs/7 weeks): Extended intervention track for repeat or more serious DWI cases.

Drug Offender Education – $80 (15 hrs/5 days): Covers illicit, prescription, and over-the-counter drug risks and use patterns.

Victim Impact Panel – $40 (2 hrs): Victim-perspective session on the consequences of impaired driving.

Drug & Alcohol Evaluation – $150: Court-approved substance-use evaluation conducted by a Licensed Chemical Dependency Counselor.

Making Better Choices (Life Skills/Decision Making) – $80 (15 hrs/5 days): Decision-making, budgeting, resume-building, and conflict-resolution skills.

Domestic Violence Prevention Program – $540 (36 hrs/18 weeks, non-CJAD): Duluth-model program modified to be non-gender-specific.

BIPP – $720 (CJAD-regulated): Now CJAD-approved for virtual/Zoom delivery, opened up specifically to serve rural counties.

Anger Management – $150 (18 hrs/12 weeks): CBT-based curriculum on triggers, escalation, and conflict resolution.

Theft Offender – $65 (12 hrs/3 days): Covers theft types, Texas Penal Code penalties, and victim impact.

Description: Multi-service provider offering all classes above in English, Spanish, and ASL, with in-person and Zoom options, payment plans, and an indigent/pro-bono policy for qualifying court- or probation-referred participants. BBB Accredited since 2023.
