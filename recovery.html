Recovery

Facility Type Key

ISF
Intermediate Sanction Facility — short-term residential placement used as a sanction for a violation of community supervision.
SATF
Substance Abuse Treatment Facility — residential treatment for chemical dependency.
DDRF
Dual Diagnosed Residential Facility — residential treatment for co-occurring mental health and substance abuse diagnoses.
CRTC
Court Residential Treatment Center — court-operated residential reentry and treatment program.
SAFPF
Substance Abuse Felony Punishment Facility — an intensive six to nine month therapeutic community program in a prison setting.
TAIP
Treatment Alternative to Incarceration Program.
