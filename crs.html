Community Service (CSR)

CSR Department Hours

You have been referred to the Johnson County CSCD Community Service Office to complete your court‑ordered Community Service hours.

Weekday Sign‑In: Report between 7:30 am – 8:00 am (8:01 am is considered late). Tardiness will be documented and reported to your CSO.

Weekday Hours:
• MON–FRI: 8 am – 12 pm (4‑hour day)
• MON–FRI: 8 am – 5 pm (8‑hour day)

Weekend Hours:
• SAT–SUN: 8 am – 5 pm (8 hours only)
• Weekend sign‑in: 7:30 am – 7:45 am (7:46 am is late)
• No half‑days offered on weekends.

Requirements: You must be on time and dressed appropriately.

Holiday Closures:
• Mon Oct 12, 2026 – Columbus Day
• Wed Nov 11, 2026 – Veterans Day
• Thu–Fri Nov 26–27, 2026 – Thanksgiving Holidays
• Thu Dec 24, 2026 – Christmas Eve
• Fri Dec 25, 2026 – Christmas Day
• Fri Jan 1, 2027 – New Year’s Day
