Community Resources for Johnson County

About This Page

This page connects you with trusted organizations across Johnson County that provide support with housing, food, mental health, medical care, and family violence services. Choose a category from the list to find helpful websites, contact information, and guidance on how to get started. These resources are available to all residents, including individuals on probation, parole, or any form of court‑ordered supervision.
