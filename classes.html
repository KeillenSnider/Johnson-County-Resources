Courses for Johnson County

About This Page

This page provides information on classes and programs that may be required by the court, probation, or parole. Each category contains links to approved providers, along with details on enrollment, costs, and how to get started. These resources are open to all residents, including individuals under court‑ordered supervision who need to complete specific classes as part of their requirements.

Community Service (CSR)

Complete court‑ordered Community Service requirements through the Johnson County CSCD CSR Department. This section provides information on reporting times, daily schedules, weekend procedures, and holiday closures to help individuals meet their mandated service obligations.

DWI Courses

Access state‑approved DWI education programs required for court, probation, or parole. Find trusted providers offering classes that meet legal requirements and help individuals complete mandated training.

Life Skills

Access court‑approved Life Skills programs designed to promote responsible decision‑making, accountability, and personal growth. These programs include courses such as Victim Impact Panels and other behavioral‑change classes required as part of probation or court‑ordered supervision.

Multi-Service Class Providers

Some providers offer several different types of court‑ordered classes under one company. This section lists those multi‑service providers so a single visit can cover more than one requirement, rather than needing to find a separate provider for each class type.

Recovery Programs

Explore evidence-based recovery programs designed to support individuals in their journey toward sobriety and wellness. These programs offer a range of services, including counseling, group therapy, and aftercare support.
