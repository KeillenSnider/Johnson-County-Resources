Courses for Johnson County

About This Page

This page provides information on classes and programs that may be required by the court, probation, or parole. Each category contains links to approved providers, along with details on enrollment, costs, and how to get started. These resources are open to all residents, including individuals under court‑ordered supervision who need to complete specific classes as part of their requirements.

DWI Courses

Access state‑approved DWI education programs required for court, probation, or parole. Find trusted providers offering classes that meet legal requirements and help individuals complete mandated training.

Recovery Programs

Explore evidence-based recovery programs designed to support individuals in their journey toward sobriety and wellness. These programs offer a range of services, including counseling, group therapy, and aftercare support.
