Adult Probation

The Johnson and Somervell Counties Community Supervision and Corrections Department is a multi-district department that provides supervision services to the courts of Johnson and Somervell counties. The Department helps ensure public safety by supervising offenders who are on community supervision. We enforce the conditions of supervision as ordered by the court and assist offenders in becoming pro-social members of the community.

Johnson County has 3 District Courts and 2 County Courts at Law. Johnson County CSCD has a staff of 60 who are responsible for carrying out duties as directed by the Judge. The department has specialized units which include Court Services, Bond, Substance Abuse and SAFPF caseloads, Sex Offender and Mental Health caseloads, and Community Service. Additionally, Somervell County is served by one County Court and two District Courts from Johnson County, which also provide judicial services to the Somervell County community.

Vision

We envision a department that has a positive impact on the community by promoting offender change that will continue beyond the term of supervision.

Mission

The Johnson and Somervell Counties CSCD is committed to protecting the public interest and safety of the community by performing high quality casework and effectively supervising individuals placed on community supervision by the courts.

Values

Commitment to those we serve.

Respect for those we supervise.

Empowerment through education and change.
