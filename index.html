About This Page

This page lists classes and community resources for anyone in Johnson County, including those under court-ordered supervision. Find court-ordered education programs, along with support for housing, food, mental health, and medical needs. Select a category above to see trusted, carefully researched organizations and websites for each.

These resources support your supervision requirements and help you stay informed. If you have questions about your specific situation, contact your supervising officer directly.
